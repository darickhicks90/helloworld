CHARACTERS (people and creatures)
Size:    16 x 16 px per frame is the house style (16 x 24 for tall folk). Up to 64 x 64.
Format:  PNG with transparency.
Frames:  a walk cycle side by side: name_f2.png = 2 frames (32 x 16 for 16 px frames).
         Draw them facing the viewer or to the right; the Builder mirrors them.
The player: the file named player (player.png or player_f2.png) is who you play.
In the Builder: give each character lines to say (one per line) and a sound for its voice,
and choose whether it wanders about.
