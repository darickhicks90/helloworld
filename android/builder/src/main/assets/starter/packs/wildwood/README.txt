WILDWOOD PACK
=============

A set of sprites in the new look: chunky, round shapes with dark outlines and clean shading
bands (lit from the upper left), mean little monsters, and a bright storybook forest.
It sits in its own folder so it never mixes with (or overwrites) your own sprites, and the
Builder lists everything here with "wildwood/" in front of the name.

  tiles/       grass, grass_flowers, grass_dark, dirt_path, dirt_pebbles, forest_floor, moss_stone
  walls/       hedge, tree_canopy, pond_water_f2, rock_wall, bramble
  props/       oak_tree, autumn_tree, blossom_tree, pine_tree (32 x 48 / 24 x 48), stump,
               fallen_log, boulder, mushrooms, glow_mushrooms_f2, root_door_sealed, locked_gate
  decor/       tall_grass_f2, fern, wildflowers, lily_pads, autumn_leaves, fireflies_f4
  characters/  imp, stalker, behemoth (4 directions, walking), and teal-and-gold "_ally"
               versions of each; five_eyed_f2 and blossom_f2 (the two bosses, front on)
  kiosks/      root_door (a way to the next level), rune_shrine, treasure_chest
  items/       key, herb, spirit_bloom, heart_fruit

To change one: open it in PIXEL STUDIO and SAVE AS; your copy goes in your own sprites
folder and the pack stays as it is. Delete this folder if you don't want it; the Builder
won't put it back unless you ask (World menu > Put back missing starter files).

Making more like these: in PIXEL STUDIO, tap MONSTER HELP. In short: RAMPS for colours,
MIRROR on, BLOB the body parts, SHADE / LIGHT to round things off, OUTLINE > Inner lines,
then OUTLINE > Dark ink. TINT makes variants (and ally colours) in one tap.
