# FoxBox Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

FoxBox does not collect, store on servers, or share any personal data.

* **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** FoxBox makes no network connections.
* **Microphone:** used only when you tap *Mic* to record a sample. The recording
  stays on your device.
* **Bluetooth and nearby devices:** used only to find and connect MIDI
  controllers you choose. FoxBox does not use your location.
* **Files:** your projects are stored inside the app. MIDI clips and audio you
  make are saved to your device's `Music/FoxBox` folder. Audio files you load
  are read only when you pick them.
* **Crash details:** if FoxBox closes unexpectedly, a crash report is saved on
  your device. It is only shared if you choose *Share details*.

Uninstalling FoxBox removes its project data. Files in `Music/FoxBox` stay
until you delete them.

Questions: contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
