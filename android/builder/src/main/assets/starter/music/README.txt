MUSIC
Format:  OGG (best), MP3 or WAV. Stereo or mono.
Length:  any; it loops. For a seamless loop, cut it exactly on a bar line.
Size:    up to 20 MB (a 3-minute OGG is about 3-4 MB).
Use:     open the Music tab and tap a track to make it this room's music (tap "None" to
         clear). Rooms with no music keep playing whatever was on.
