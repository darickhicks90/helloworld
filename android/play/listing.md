## Full description
5 Eye Yeti is a pocket music studio with a 16-bit cottagecore heart. Write loops
with up to six swappable 5IY Blocks, record audio, then perform it all in
Clibbins and mix it down to tape.

WRITE IN THE STUDIO
• Gusts: a warm analog-style synth
• Reflections: a sampler with smooth time-stretch. Record your voice or load any sound
• Leaves: a granular synth that grows clouds of sound
• Minds: drums with a generative brain. Draw a beat, then let it dream
• Bumble: a buzzy bass with a honeycomb step sequencer that follows your chords
• Flowers: design your own effects (chorus, flanger, reverb, delay, rhythmic
  gates and a modulation matrix)

PLAY WITHOUT WRONG NOTES
Pick a key and scale. Tap chords or hold one and strum. Bend the sound with an
XY pad you can map to any parameter.

PERFORM IN CLIBBINS
Your clips and audio takes lie scattered in the grass. Tap them to queue the
next scene and it changes on the beat. Draw daisy chains from your flowers
through clips to run them through effects, set volume and pan, then arm the
master and mix the whole song down to tape.

FOR MUSICIANS
• USB and Bluetooth MIDI, MPE (ROLI Seaboard), pad controllers (Synido TempoPad)
• Record loops with count-in, metronome and quantize
• Every clip is saved as a MIDI file, and your performances as audio, in
  Music/5IY
• Low-latency audio (AAudio)

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your music stays on your device.
