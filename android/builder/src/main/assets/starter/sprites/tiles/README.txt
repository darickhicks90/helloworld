FLOOR TILES (walkable)
Size:    exactly 16 x 16 px (other sizes are stretched to fit a cell).
Format:  PNG. Usually fully opaque.
Use:     pick it in the Tiles tab and paint with your finger; Erase clears a cell.
Tips:    make the edges match so tiles join without seams. Two or three variants of the
         same floor (marble.png, marble_veined.png) mixed together look less repetitive.
Animated tiles: frames side by side, name ending _f2, _f3 ... (32 x 16 for _f2).
