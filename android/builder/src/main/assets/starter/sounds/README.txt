SOUND EFFECTS
Format:  WAV (16-bit PCM) or OGG. Mono is plenty.
Rate:    22050, 44100 or 48000 Hz.
Length:  keep them short, under about 3 seconds (1 MB at most).
Volume:  normalise to around -3 dB; quiet files stay quiet.
Use:     tap one in the Sounds tab to hear it. With an object selected, tap a sound to give
         it to that object (a character's voice, a kiosk's chime, a pickup's jingle).
Names:   pickup.wav and step.wav are used for pickups and footsteps if they exist.
